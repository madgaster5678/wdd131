Welcome to Void Core Game Design

We welcome you to Void Core Game Design, an online website for budding game developers to share their work and get help in making their dreams for making their own game come true.

What We Offer

Here we offer many resources to help anyone, both those with experience and those without. Here are some of the things we offer:

  • Help with creating ideas for games
  • Links to those with expertise in both character and level design
  • Help in understanding the code behide making a game

Why Join Us?

We here at Void Core Game Design are a collection of experienced game developers and know how hard it can be trying to make one of your own. Whether it comes to finding help in character design or building a level; it can be tough know what to do.

  • We know many people with experience in both character and level design
  • We have come across almost every issue when it come to the programming of a game
  • Many people, from friends and family, to other indie devs have used our resources and liked what we shared with them
