About Void Core

Void Core Game Design was built by me, Ammon Griner, and my friends Dante Sparda, Trish Rush, and Nero Sparda, Dante's nephew. We came together when I, at the time a budding game developer, needed help with both character and level design. Dante and Trish had some experience with both but we needed more help. Nero knew some others that could help. After my game launched I included some info about how I made it. Fans of my game said that my friends and I should try to help others in game development. That is how we came to be.

Our Community

Our community is smaller than most people believe it to be. This is mostly because not all developers want help when making their game. But those who have joined us here have found a great place to learn and grow. We have received many emails from those in our community about the friendliness of the community and the great help they have received. We also make sure to verify any artists or developers to make sure they have the experience they claim to.
